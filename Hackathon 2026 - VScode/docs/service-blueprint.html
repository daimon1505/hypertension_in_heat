Where it breaks
Consent obtained once, then never revisited. People change their minds.
An incomplete medication list leaves the score uncertain — and uncertainty must be shown, not hidden.
No forecast means no priority. The service has to stay useful with the feed down.
The person most at risk may have no smartphone, no data, or no English.
Advice a patient cannot act on: no AC, no transport, no cool space open nearby.
A dashboard flag is not proof anyone read it. Delivery must be tracked separately.
Alert fatigue. Too many days flagged and the next one gets ignored.

The decision at the centre

Two inputs, one assessment

Everything in both maps converges here. H describes the weather; V describes the person. They meet in a matrix and are never added together.

Routine: maintain the heat plan. Targeted: personalised prevention and support checks. Priority: flag for care-team review before heat exposure — not an emergency diagnosis.

V · five domains, 0–2 points each

  • AClinical: age 65+, heart failure, CKD, diabetes, past heat illnesscap 2
  • BMedication: 2 for a diuretic with an ACE inhibitor, ARB or ARNI; 1 for any heat-relevant drughighest only
  • CCooling: reliable at home, alternative only, or neitherchoose one
  • DExposure: heat exposure without breaks, or strenuous activity in heatchoose one
  • ESupport: no reliable check-in person; needs help to follow the planadd both

Maximum 10 points. Not a percentage, and not a probability of illness. Hypertension is the eligibility criterion, not a scored factor.

Honest gaps

What would have to be true in the real world

A live HeatRisk feed

The demo uses synthetic heat levels. The real product needs verified ingestion of the official product, with its version and timestamp stored.

Pharmacist sign-off

Every drug-heat flag currently says UNREVIEWED. Nothing reaches a patient until that changes.

Weights that have been tested

Points, caps and matrix cells are design choices informed by evidence, not calibrated against outcomes.

A way to reach everyone

The people at highest risk are least likely to be reachable through an app. Phone and translated messages are not optional extras.

Somewhere cool to send people

Advice only works if a cool space is open, reachable and free. That is a city partnership, not a feature.

Proof of delivery

Track that an alert was received and acted on, separately from it being displayed.
